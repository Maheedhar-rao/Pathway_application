Credit Setup

Step 6 of 7

Soft credit pull — won't affect your score

We use IDIQ to perform a soft inquiry on your credit. A soft pull is only visible to you — it does not appear to other lenders and has no impact on your credit score. This is different from a hard inquiry, which would temporarily lower your score and remain visible on your report.

Creating an IDIQ account lets us retrieve the report securely and gives you ongoing access to your own credit monitoring.

How it works

  1. Click Open IDIQ Signup to create an account in a new tab.
  2. After signup, return here and click Next to enter your IDIQ login.
